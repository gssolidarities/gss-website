The Visual Archive

Frames.

Justice-driven manga and documentary film — a curated room where the panel and the reel keep what the archive sheds. Each title a long look at empire, labour, memory and refusal.

Enter the room ↓ What this is

Two forms. One archival ethic.

The panel and the reel do what the register will not — they stay with a face, a street, a wound, a shift. They let the reader linger inside a frame instead of past it, and they refuse the aerial view that lets history look clean.

Every work here was chosen for that insistence. We keep manga drawn from inside survival — Hiroshima, Occupied Korea, Showa-era empire — and documentary cinema that films the long now of colonial violence: mines, prisons, borders, kitchens, amnesia. Nothing comic in the easy sense. Everything serious in the exact sense: at stake.

If you hold a title we should keep, write to us.
