The record

Sourced, argued, and open to revision

Factual scenes above are attributed to the sources below; the closing scene is an explicitly labelled interpretive reading — this project's argument, kept apart from the record it reads. Quotations are verbatim from the cited primary pages.

Method: camera choreography and pins are generated from a versioned scene file; sub-landmark coordinates are tagged APPROX on the map and must not be read as precise. Citations flagged “pending verification” link to the holder's real root domain until the exact URL is confirmed. Full standards: the map's METHOD panel. Built on MapLibre GL JS — open source, no API keys.
