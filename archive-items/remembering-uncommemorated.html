§ 01 · Why this shelf

Some dead go on the record.
Most do not.

The public record of atrocity is not neutral. It is a highly curated shelf. On that shelf are events the wealthy West has consented to remember — with dates, memorials, museum wings, ministerial visits, and permanent syllabus entries.

Off that shelf are events of the same or greater magnitude for which the machinery of remembrance was never built. The names are known to survivors, families, and to a scattered scholarly literature. They are largely unknown to the school textbook, the evening news, and the national memorial calendar.

This sub-archive is a small, specific room within the GSS Anarchive — reserved for those uncommemorated genocides and holocausts. Each entry here is an interactive data page — sourced, cited, animated — presenting one rigorous body of dissenting research at its full scale.

“Holocaust ignored yields holocaust repeated.”
